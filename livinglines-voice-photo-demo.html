Voice-note ordering

For masons, plumbers and contractors who send a voice note instead of typing.
  • Voice note receivedContractor sends a Telugu/English voice note on WhatsApp.
  • Speech to textMixed Telugu, Hindi and English is transcribed, including trade slang.
  • Matched to catalog SKUs"Elbow", "CP tap", "bag" become real product codes, sizes and units.
  • Quote built with stock checkPrice, total and availability come back in seconds.
  • Staff approve, customer confirmsOne tap for staff. Low-confidence items get flagged.
Why it matters: The customers who spend the most on materials are the least likely to type a message. Quoting takes staff 10 to 15 minutes today. This takes seconds.

Photo-to-quote

Customer sends a photo of a design they like, or a competitor's quote.
  • Photo receivedBathroom inspiration, a tile design, or a competitor's quote.
  • Visual search of the catalogThe AI reads style, colour, size and finish, and finds the closest products in stock.
  • Competitor quote comparisonIf it's a quote, the AI reads the line items and offers equivalents.
  • Options sent with priceBest match, a budget option, and a premium option.
  • Showroom visit bookedCustomer gets a sample or visit slot at the nearest branch.
Why it matters: "Do you have this?" is the most common unanswered question in a showroom. Every photo that goes unanswered is a customer who walks to a competitor.
